They keep showing up late to your one-on-ones. And acting like it’s no big deal.

This is hard because it can feel too small to name. But when you let it slide, you teach them your time is flexible and your meetings with them are optional.

Three sentences that help:

1. “I want to talk about our one-on-ones. You’ve been late more than once.”
2. “When that happens, we lose time we set aside for your work and your growth.”
3. “What needs to change so we start on time from here?”

Name the pattern, not their character. “Late to our one-on-ones” is useful. “Unprofessional” starts a fight.

Small things get big when nobody says them out loud.
