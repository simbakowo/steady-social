The same two people talk over everyone in team meetings.
And the quieter people have stopped trying.

This is hard because the loud voices are not trying to be rude. They think they are helping. But if you never name it, the rest of the team learns that airtime is not shared here.

Three sentences that help:

1. “I want to talk about what happens in our team meetings when others get cut off.”
2. “I’ve noticed you jump in fast, and it means some people do not finish their point.”
3. “In the next meeting, I need you to pause and let one more person speak before you come in.”

Talk about the meeting behavior, not their personality. “Too intense” starts a fight. “You cut people off” is something they can change.

You are not asking them to be smaller. You are asking them to make room.
