Your friend now reports to you. And their work has slipped.

This is the conversation new managers put off the longest. You were peers a month ago. Now you have to say the thing nobody else will.

Three sentences that help:

1. “I want to talk about the last two reports. This is me as your manager, not as your friend.”
2. “Here is what I saw. Tell me what I’m missing.”
3. “Here is what I need by Friday. What would get in the way?”

Say the topic in the first sentence. They should never have to guess why you asked to talk.

It will feel awkward. It is less awkward than them hearing it from someone else in six months.
